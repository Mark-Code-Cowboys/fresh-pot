# Fresh Pot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** (set at publication)
**Publisher:** Code Cowboys LLC
**Published at:** https://code-cowboys.com/privacy/freshpot

Fresh Pot is a personal coffee journal. Our privacy policy is short
because the app is built so that we *cannot* see your data.

## The short version

Everything you record in Fresh Pot — beans, brews, ratings, tasting
notes, photos, your gear — is stored **only on your device**. Fresh
Pot has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no
network calls of its own. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell,
or share any personal data. We could not hand your journal to anyone
even if asked, because we never have it.

## Bag-label scanning

The bag scanner photographs coffee bags and reads them **on your
device** (Google ML Kit's bundled, offline text recognition). Label
images and everything read from them stay on your phone. Nothing is
uploaded — there is nowhere to upload it to.

## Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you scan a bag
label or attach a photo to a bag. Images you capture are stored
locally with the rest of your journal.

## Importing

The CSV importer reads a file you choose from your device. Its
contents go into your local journal and nowhere else.

## Sharing, export, and backup

CSV export and the backup file are created only when you tap the
button, and go only where you send them via your phone's share sheet.
Restoring a backup reads a file you choose. We are never in that path.

## Purchases

Fresh Pot Pro is sold through Google Play / the App Store. The stores
process payment and tell the app only "this purchase is owned." We
receive no payment details and keep no purchase records of our own.

## Children

Fresh Pot does not knowingly collect data from anyone — including
children — because it collects no data at all.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes what leaves your device, this policy
and its effective date will change with it, and the app will say so
before anything new happens.
